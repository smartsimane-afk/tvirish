Your complete satisfaction is our #1 commitment. Here's our transparent, hassle-free refund policy for your IPTV Ireland subscription.

Every TVirish Irish IPTV plan includes a full 30-day money-back guarantee, wherever in Ireland you're subscribing from.

130-Day Money-Back Guarantee

All TVirish Irish IPTV subscription plans (1 Month, 12 Months, 24 Months, and Multi-Screen plans) are covered by a full money-back guarantee for the first 30 days after purchase. If you experience an unresolvable technical issue, or the channel lineup doesn't meet your reasonable expectations, we will refund you in full.

2Eligibility Requirements

  • Your request must be submitted within 30 days of the initial activation date.

3How to Request a Refund

By Email support@iptvismarts.com
